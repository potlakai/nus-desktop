Nūs Desktop 0.2.8. It opens at once, answers in one go, and knows you.

**It opens at once.** The dashboard appears the moment you start Nūs. The sign-in check and the calendar refresh used to run first, so a slow connection, or no connection at all, left you looking at nothing. Now they run in the background after the window is up, each with a short time limit, and the screen fills in as they finish. With no internet you still get your semester straight away.

**Ask once, get the answer.** A question in the Ask bar, the Knot chat or by voice is now one call to your AI instead of two. The reply streams in as it is written, and when you ask Nūs to do something, such as add a task or move a due date, the confirm card comes out of that same reply. Each question now uses one of your questions for the day, not two. If you use Claude Code, Nūs keeps a process ready ahead of your next question and runs it with its tools switched off, so it can only answer, never touch your files.

**A card for what Nūs knows about you.** Settings has a new "What Nūs knows about you" card. Add your name, school, major, year and the courses you take, or tell the Knot to remember something, and every answer is written with that in mind. The facts stay on this computer and go nowhere but your own AI. Card and identity numbers are kept private and never put in a prompt. There is a one-click prompt to bring over what ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini already knows about you, and a button to remove it again.

**Fixed.** Nūs no longer opens a second dashboard window behind the first on start.

Windows only. Mac builds come from the build-mac workflow. The installer is still unsigned, so Windows will ask: choose More info, then Run anyway.
