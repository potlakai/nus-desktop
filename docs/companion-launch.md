# Nūs Companion launch experiment

Status: draft materials, not published or scheduled. Start only after the release gates in companion-restoration.md pass. The founder approved Claude for explicitly selected screen areas; that connection is implemented in source and mock-tested, but not yet verified live or released. Do not film or advertise it as shipped until it passes the live checks.

## Positioning

X introduces Nūs Companion through the Knot and a useful desktop interaction. Instagram introduces the same product through a concrete school outcome. Neither channel should promise autonomous computer control, universal capture invisibility, or exclusive capabilities without evidence.

The Knot is the visual hook; helping someone complete a real next step is the payoff. Keep its corner home visible before and after the interaction. The person performs the clicks.

## Six X drafts, two weeks

Use days 1, 3, 5, 8, 10 and 12 relative to a founder-approved launch date. The founder reviews and publishes; no automated posting is configured.

Attach a short, sound-off-readable hook clip and a link to an uninterrupted proof recording. Use a 10–15-second hook as a test, not a platform rule. Label edited or sped-up footage. Never substitute a fake pointing fixture for model-guided behavior.

Each clip ends with one appropriate action: try the validated build, or join the waitlist if release gates remain open. Do not invent a download URL or imply availability on an untested platform.

## Instagram brief

Show one school problem per post: understand an assignment, decide the next action, get through the work. Open with the problem and close with an observable result. Do not lead with the entire app dashboard. Avoid claims about grades or learning outcomes without evidence.

## Measurement

Use the existing landing page with campaign tags: utm_source=x or instagram; utm_medium=organic_social; utm_campaign=companion_launch; utm_content=x01 through x06. Confirm these tags are supported by existing attribution before publishing. No new surveillance or background capture.

Record per post: qualified visits, downloads/signups, first useful companion interaction, seven-day return use, and reported problems. Use existing consented analytics and interviews. Views alone do not select a winner; compare X variants with each other and treat a six-post sample as directional only.

Ask testers whether they understand that Nūs guides while they act, whether the Knot distracts them, and whether they would leave it enabled. Record task and app versions with each observation. Do not report invented participants or results.

## Evidence boundaries

These conclusions are carried forward from the founder-approved research brief, not a new benchmark of competitors' private implementations. Public behavior and documentation support design lessons; they do not reveal private APIs or prove that one interface caused virality.

| Reference | Retained product lesson | Source |
